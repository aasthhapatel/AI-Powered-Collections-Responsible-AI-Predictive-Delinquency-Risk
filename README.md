# AI-Powered Collections — Responsible AI & Predictive Delinquency Risk
A practical end-to-end case study focused on designing a responsible AI-powered collections system. The project covers exploratory data analysis, delinquency risk assessment, predictive modeling strategy, agentic AI decision workflows, responsible AI guardrails, explainability, fairness, human-in-the-loop oversight, and business impact measurement.

The solution demonstrates how AI can help financial institutions identify customers at risk of delinquency, prioritize proactive interventions, and support collections teams without replacing human judgment.

Key areas: Predictive Analytics • Machine Learning • Generative/Agentic AI • Responsible AI • Explainable AI • Risk Scoring • Collections Optimization • Human-in-the-Loop AI
